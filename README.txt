Inspection templates are based on Wieger's published checklists. They have been converted to markdowns and optimized for Obsidian-based note-taking and report preparation. 
- Optionally, for better formatting, after opening the folder in Obsidian, place the included inspection-forms.css under .obsidian/snippets, and enable it under Settings (bottom left cog) --> Appearance --> CSS snippets.
- You can generate PDF versions by clicking top left three dots --> Export to PDF.
