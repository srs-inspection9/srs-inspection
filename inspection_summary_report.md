# Inspection Summary Report

## Inspection Identification

- **Project:** Team Nexus, Group 9 SRS Inspection
- **Meeting Date:** 2026, November 24th

## Work Product Description

The work product under inspection was Group 9's Software Requirements Specification draft for the SNU Faculty of Science Research Grants Management System. The document was provided in MD format including six main sections and supporting appendices, covering system scope, user characteristics, functional requirements, use cases, data requirements, external interfaces, security requirements, business rules and stakeholder evidence. The inspection focused on evaluating the completeness, consistency, clarity, traceability and verifiability of these stated requirements listed.

## Inspection Process Description

- Group 9's SRS draft distributed among the four members of Team Nexus for review and inspection.
- Roles were assigned collaboratively, Arvin worked as Moderator and Inspector, Roman as Reader and Recorder with Harriet and Sahar as Inspectors.
- Each member reviewed the SRS and identified potential defects, inconsistencies and incomplete requirements.
- The group spent approximately three hours collectively preparing the meeting script, organizing individual findings and compiling the Inspection Issue Log fully.
- The inspection meeting was then conducted and recorded through Zoom on November 24, 2026, lasting for approximately 30 minutes of live recording time.
- The Moderator led the meeting, managed transitions between sections and encouraged participation from all group members.
- The Reader summarized the relevant sections of the SRS, allowing inspectors to present their findings for each section.
- The group discussed the location, classification, severity and potential impact of the identified issues for every section consistently.
- The Recorder documented the findings in the shared Inspection Issue Log on GitHub later on.
- The inspection covered the six main sections of the SRS and relevant supporting appendices, with a main attention given to functional requirements, business rules and stakeholder evidence.
- The group discussed the product appraisal approval and prepared the final inspection deliverables following the meeting.

## Summary of Issues

The inspection recorded 45 issues, with both major and minor inconsistencies, incomplete requirements, ambiguities, unverifiable requirements and untraceable requirements. The most significant issues consisted of inconsistent application statuses, unclear approval and correction workflows, grant-cycle deadlines, conflicting access permissions and incomplete data retention requirements. Recurring issues also consisted of insufficient stakeholder evidence, undefined system dependencies and inconsistencies between business rules and functional requirements. These findings may affect the functionality, security and testability of the proposed system and should be addressed before further developments and finalizations can then take place.
